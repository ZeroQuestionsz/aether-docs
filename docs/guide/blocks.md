# Blocks & screens

Three blocks bring the map into your base, and LCD apps put AETHER on any screen.

## Blocks

| Block | What it does |
|---|---|
| AETHER Command Console | A large main screen plus three side screens (map, satellites, contacts, alerts). <kbd>F</kbd> opens the star map; its main screen mirrors the map live and keeps the view you left. |
| AETHER Holo Table | The star map as a 3D hologram above the table, with labels and three rim screens. <kbd>F</kbd> on its front panel. |
| AETHER Star Map Terminal | A small terminal. <kbd>F</kbd> opens the star map. |

## LCD apps

On any screen of a grid with an AETHER block (or on a registered fleet ship), choose **Edit LCD → Content: Script** and pick one: Star Map, Satellite Status, Contacts, Alerts, Local Radar, Selected Object, Docking, Docking Steps.

## Cockpit screens

Drag the cockpit onto its own toolbar (<kbd>G</kbd> menu) for the actions **AETHER: next screen**, **next / previous app** and **show &lt;app&gt;**. Switch apps from the seat; cycling past the last app returns the screen to what it showed before.

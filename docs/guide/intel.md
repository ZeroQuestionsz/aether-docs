# Intelligence & alerts

Everything your satellites, antennas and you see goes into an intelligence database, and AETHER tells you when something needs your attention.

## Contacts

A contact's colour is how you stand with its owner: your reputation with NPC factions (hostile at -500 or less, friendly at 500 or more), your faction, or the game's own relation for players. Its info panel names the owner and why. Your registered fleet always shows as yours.

A contact is **live** while something of yours can see it and **stale** once nothing can. Stale contacts keep their last known position, trail and 3D shape, and expire after 24 game hours (configurable per world).

## Exploration levels

Each body is **Known**, **Visited**, **Mapped** or **Surveyed**. Measurements taken on site (oxygen, air density, gravity, weather) become verified values. AETHER warns you if a jump drive counts down towards unexplored space.

## Alerts

| Alert | When |
|---|---|
| New contact | A grid seen for the first time, back in view, or lost. |
| Watch zone | A contact inside or heading into a zone around a GPS marker you choose (5, 20 or 50 km). |
| Inbound | A hostile heading straight at you, with time to contact. |
| Intercept | A hostile whose path passes close to yours. |
| Shadowing / closing | A hostile keeping pace with you, or closing in. |
| Satellite health | Low battery or fuel, offline, no gyros, pointing fault, drifting, in gravity. |

Alerts appear in chat, on screen, in the map header and console, and JARVIS can read them.

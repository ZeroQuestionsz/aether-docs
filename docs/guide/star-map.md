# Star map

A 3D map of what you know: planets and moons, your satellites, your GPS markers and every grid AETHER has seen.

## Three views

**Solar System view** shows every body, lit from the Sun's real direction. Zoom into a planet and the map slides smoothly into **Planet View**, with real terrain relief, atmosphere haze and drifting clouds. Zoom in all the way, or press <kbd>G</kbd>, for [Ground view](./ground-view.md): the terrain around you or any spot. <kbd>Up</kbd> and <kbd>Down</kbd> step between the three. The view you leave is remembered per world.

The game HUD hides while the map is open and returns exactly as it was. The map is drawn in front of everything, so walls and your helmet never cover it.

## What's on it

| Marker | Meaning |
|---|---|
| You | Your character or the ship you're in. |
| Satellites | Your antennas in Satellite mode, always named. Select one to see a 3D model coloured by block type. |
| Contacts | Grids AETHER has detected, coloured by how you stand with the owner (your reputation with NPC factions, as the game's HUD shows it). Moving ones show a dashed line to where they'll be in 60 seconds. |
| GPS markers | Your GPS entries. Rename them and change their colour from the map. |

Double-click a satellite, contact or GPS marker to follow it with the camera. Where several markers sit on top of each other, a click opens a small list to pick the one you mean.

## The info panel

Select anything for its details. A planet's panel adds an **assessment**: gravity, air, oxygen, temperature, weather, expected ores, terrain, hostile activity and hazards. Each line is tagged:

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `V` | Verified: measured on site. |
| `E` | Estimated from what AETHER knows. |
| `S` | Stale: true when last seen. |
| `?` | Unknown. |
